# v1.22.0 — The Bestiary Comes Alive

Forty monsters. One hundred sixty items. All the AI-generated placeholders from before v1.21 are replaced with curated content. The infrastructure has been ready since v1.21.0 (zone-aware spawning) and v1.21.1 (HP-pool multi-turn combat); v1.22 is the content drop those releases were waiting for.

---

## 🐛 The Bestiary, Filled Out

The Horror and Boss-adjacent tiers were placeholders in v1.21 (high-danger zones gracefully fell back to Hunter tier). They're real now. **40 monsters across 5 tiers, 8 per tier**, drafted by a writing-category agent and tuned to the HP/ATK/XP ranges from `docs/balance-analysis.md` §6.1.

### Tier 1 — Vermin (zones 1-2)
Small filesystem creatures, single-hit kills, low XP. The intro tier.

```
Symlink Slug · Dotfile Dust-Mite · Cache Cricket · Tempfile Tadpole
Log-File Larva · Socket Snail · Inode Inchworm · Permission Pupa
```

### Tier 2 — Bruiser (zones 2-3)
Process spirits, fight-back energy, 2-3 hit kills.

```
Zombie Process Zealot · Background Job Brawler · Daemon Duelist · Shell-Script Shaman
Pipe-Line Pugilist · Env-Var Vandal · Cron-Job Crusader · Std-Error Specter
```

### Tier 3 — Hunter (zones 3-4)
Memory dwellers with predator aesthetic, 3-5 hit kills.

```
Heap-Alloc Hound · Stack-Trace Stalker · Pointer Panther · Overflow Basilisk
Garbage-Collector Gryphon · Memory-Leak Manticore · Segfault Shark · Dangling-Pointer Dingo
```

### Tier 4 — Horror (zones 4-5)
Kernel terrors, serious threats, 5-8 hit kills.

```
Kernel-Panic Kraken · Interrupt-Handler Hydra · System-Call Siren · Page-Fault Phantom
Deadlock Dragon · Race-Condition Reaper · Root-Kit Revenant · Scheduler-Shadow Stalker
```

### Tier 5 — Boss-adjacent (zones 5+)
Architectural entities, the kind you flee. 10+ hit kills.

```
The Monolith Manifestation · Architectural-Debt Archon · The Legacy-Code Lich
Distributed-System Djinn · The Microservice Medusa · Cloud-Native Chimera
The Immutable-State Idol · Entropy-Engine Elemental
```

The 5 named bosses (Kernel Panic, Lord of /dev/null, SIGKILL Supreme, The Infinite Loop, The Memory Corruption) are **unchanged** — they're a separate roster from this bestiary and continue to spawn at their existing 1/500 rate via `boss::maybe_spawn`.

## ⚔️ Walking Into `node_modules` Now Means Something

Before v1.22, the danger-5 zone (`node_modules`) fell back to Hunter tier because Horror and Boss-adjacent slots were empty. Now those slots are populated.

```
$ cd ~/projects/myrepo/node_modules
$ rm -rf some_orphan_thing
🪤  A Deadlock Dragon ascends from the depths!  (HP 150, ATK 22)
...
⚔️  The Deadlock Dragon falls! Glorious victory! +130 XP
```

A level-100 character will need 3-5 turns to kill a Boss-adjacent. A level-30 character will probably die. **Don't `cd node_modules` casually anymore.**

## 💎 Loot Table, Reframed

160 items across the 5 rarities × 4 slots × 8 items grid. Naming intensity scales with rarity:

**Common** is **mundane**: Rusty Crowbar, Cardboard Box, Soda Tab, Lukewarm Water. The bottom rung; you'll sell most of these via `sq sell junk`.

**Uncommon** is **decent**: Polished Wrench, Reinforced Vest, Silver Band, Fresh Espresso. Real tools. Real protein.

**Rare** is the **named tools tier** — single-word evocative concepts: Bit-Blitzer, Logic-Lash, Kernel-Cloak, Code-Cordial.

**Epic** is **darker fantasy** — multi-word with attitude: Void-Pointer Voulge, Mantle of the Mainframe, Sigil of the Sysadmin, Essence of the Epoch.

**Legendary** is the **"The X-of-Y" construction** — the kind of name that makes you sit up when it drops: The Final Commit of Fate, The Aegis of the Absolute Path, The Loop of the Lost Link, The Tear of the Tired Tech-Lead.

```
$ cd ~ && sq shop
🏪 The Terminal Bazaar
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Dull Letter Opener (+1 Weapon) [Common] — 10 gold
  2. Cardboard Box (+2 Armor) [Common] — 15 gold
  3. Stained Apron (+3 Armor) [Common] — 20 gold
  4. Soda Tab (+4 Ring) [Common] — 25 gold
  5. Half-Empty Juice Box (+3 Potion) [Common] — 20 gold
```

Prices reflect the v1.19 multiplier ladder (5/8/13/22/35). The flagship "max-enchant a Legendary for 30,000 gold" math from v1.18 still holds — your Legendary just has a way better name now.

## ⚙️ How This Shipped Without Disruption

This is the *content* release. The mechanics were already in place:

- v1.21.0 added the `MonsterTier` enum + zone gating
- v1.21.1 added HP pools + multi-turn combat
- v1.22.0 swaps the actual content

Each release was small enough to ship cleanly on its own. The whole bestiary overhaul took four releases, each with its own user-facing release notes.

The writing-category agent that drafted the 40 monsters + 160 items did so as a parallel team member during v1.21 development. Designer reviewed and approved the proposal at `docs/v1.22-bestiary-naming-proposal.md`. v1.22 is a string-substitution layer on top of v1.21's infrastructure — zero schema changes, zero combat-math changes.

---

## ⚙ Compatibility

**Saves are unchanged. No schema migration. No CLI changes.**

If your save references old monsters by name (it doesn't — names are runtime strings, not save data) or old items by name (it does — every item in your inventory is a name-tagged record), **your old items remain in your inventory**. The Pike of Ping you found before v1.22 is still a Pike of Ping. New drops will come from the new pool.

A future `sq sell legacy` command (not in this release) could let you sell off pre-v1.22 items in bulk. For now, your old items mingle with new ones until you sell or replace them.
