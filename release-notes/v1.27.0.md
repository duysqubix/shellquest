# v1.27.0 — Watch Both Health Bars at Once

In the arena you could always see the monster's HP ticking down on every swing — but to check your *own* health you had to wait for the enemy to land a hit and read it off their line. By then it might be too late to cash out. Now your HP rides along on every attack you make.

---

## ❤ Your HP, On Every Swing

Each player-attack line that shows the monster's `(HP: …)` now also carries your own HP, marked with a distinct `❤` and color-tiered like a real health bar — **green** when you're healthy, **yellow** when you're hurt, **red** when you're one bad roll from a knockout. The monster's bar stays red; yours stands apart so you can read both at a glance, mid-fight, without doing the math.

```
   Your blade bites deep into the Deadlock Demon for 78 damage! (HP: 76/154) ❤ 200/200
   The Deadlock Demon strikes with fury! You take 58 damage! HP: 142/200
   CRITICAL! Your blade cleaves the Deadlock Demon for 160 damage! (HP: 0/154) ❤ 142/200
```

Watch the overlay update in real time: you open at `❤ 200/200`, the Demon clips you for 58, and your very next swing already reads `❤ 142/200`. The danger is no longer hidden between the lines — it's right there on the blow you just landed, so the Continue-or-Cash-Out call is one you make with both bars in view.
