# v1.26.0 — The Arena Tells You If You're Actually Ahead

The cash-out preview always showed you the pot — but not whether the pot had cleared your entry fee yet. You could bank "+72 gold" feeling like a winner, having forgotten you paid 600 to walk in. Now every cash-out line shows your **net** — exactly what you'd walk away with, fee and all.

---

## 💰 Net Gold in the Cash-Out Preview

After each won round the gauntlet now appends your net position: **total gold earned this run minus the entry fee.** Negative while you're still digging out of the fee, then it climbs into the green as you push deeper. The decision to Continue or Cash Out is finally an honest one — you can see whether bailing right now actually leaves you ahead.

```
   Round 1 cleared! Current HP: 600/600
   💰 Cash Out now: +72 gold, +250 XP (net: -528 gold)
   1) Continue
   2) Cash Out
```

That `-528` is the truth the old line hid: a 600-gold entry fee against 72 gold in the pot. Keep clearing rounds and watch it recover in real time:

```
   💰 Cash Out now: +72 gold, +250 XP (net: -528 gold)
   💰 Cash Out now: +180 gold, +600 XP (net: -420 gold)
   💰 Cash Out now: +330 gold, +1200 XP (net: -270 gold)
   💰 Cash Out now: +540 gold, +2000 XP (net: -60 gold)
```

The net is **color-coded** — red while you're underwater, green once the run has paid for itself, dimmed at exactly break-even. No mental math mid-fight, no nasty surprise when the gold actually lands in your purse. The number you see is the number you keep.
